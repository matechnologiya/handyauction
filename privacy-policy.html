Handyauction Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Handyauction provides horse auction services through WhatsApp and our auction management applications.

Information We Collect

We may collect information such as your name, phone number, auction registration information, bidding activity, payment information, and information related to horses you purchase.

How We Use Information

We use this information to register bidders, manage auctions, process bids and payments, communicate auction information, and provide our services.

WhatsApp

We use WhatsApp to communicate with registered bidders, including auction information, bidding notifications, and transaction-related messages.

Payments

Payments and deposits may be processed through our payment provider. We do not store your complete payment card or banking credentials.

Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with our services and limit access to authorized personnel.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact Handyauction through the contact information provided by our business.
